Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Short answers about this demo and how trips will work. Anything the team has not approved yet is marked.

Is this a real booking?

No. The estimate is a sample. No money is collected, no seats are reserved and nothing is sent to Trippy Trail. Real departures and payments will be added once the team publishes them.

How do I ask about a trip?

Use “Plan an escape” and fill in the short form. The page prepares a message that you can copy and send through the Trippy Trail Instagram page. It does not send anything for you.

How are the prices worked out?

Each trip has a sample price per person for Standard and Comfort stays. The estimate multiplies it by the number of travellers. For 8 or more travellers it applies a sample 5% group saving. Taxes and extras still need confirmation.

What is the difference between Standard and Comfort?

They are two sample stay categories on each trip. The trip details show the proposed stay and room sharing for each. The team confirms the final arrangement before anything is booked.

How hard are the walks?

Where a trip involves walking, the trip details describe the level, so you can judge it before you ask for a date. Please tell the team about any mobility or health needs when you enquire.

Will there be a bonfire?

Each trip page describes the proposed bonfire evening. It depends on the weather, the property and local permissions, so it is never guaranteed.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

The team has not approved one yet, so none is published. It will appear under Policies below before real bookings open.

Are the photos from Trippy Trail trips?

Not yet. They are web photographs of the destinations, shown as inspiration, with credits in the footer. Original Trippy Trail photos will replace them when they are supplied.

Where are my reviews and photos stored?

In this demo, only in your browser on this device. They are not sent to Trippy Trail and not published online. Clearing your browser data removes them.
