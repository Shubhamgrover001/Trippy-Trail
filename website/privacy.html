01

Who we are

Trippy Trail is a small travel brand that plans group trips to places like Shangarh, Jibhi & Shoja and Kashmir. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or contact us, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

When we say “we”, “us” or “Trippy Trail”, we mean the team behind this website. You can reach us at Trippytrail.in@gmail.com or on +91 89209 33972.

02

What we collect

We keep this to the minimum needed to plan and run your trip.

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, preferred departure date, number of travellers, and any notes you type into an enquiry or booking.
  • Messages you send us: when you contact us on WhatsApp, by call or email, we receive the details you share, such as your phone number and message.
  • Reviews and photos you choose to share: the name you give, your rating, your words and any photos you upload.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment provider. We receive a payment status and reference, never your full card, UPI PIN or net-banking details.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, our hosting provider may log your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.
03

What stays on your device

Some features work entirely inside your browser and are not sent to us:

  • Trips you save with the heart button.
  • Your colour theme choice (light, dark or system).
  • Reviews you write and photos you add while previewing the site, which are kept in your browser storage on this device.

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. If we add website analytics later, we will update this page first. You can clear the stored data any time from your browser settings.

04

Why we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and help you choose a trip.
  • To plan, confirm and run your booking, including arranging stays, transport and local partners.
  • To take payments and send confirmations or receipts.
  • To keep you informed about your trip, such as changes caused by weather or road conditions.
  • To publish reviews and photos you chose to share.
  • To keep the site secure and meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We only send promotional messages if you ask for them, and every message will tell you how to stop.

05

Your consent and legal basis

We handle your personal data in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We rely on your consent, which you give when you submit a form or message us, and on the need to carry out a booking you asked for. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us, and we will stop using your information for that purpose, unless we must keep it by law.

06

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to deliver your trip:

  • Stays, drivers and local partners who host or transport your group, limited to names, group size and dates.
  • Our payment provider to process payments securely.
  • Hosting and technology providers that keep this website running.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

Where partners are in or outside India, we share only what they need and expect them to protect it.

07

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed to run your trip and to meet tax, accounting and legal requirements. Enquiries that do not become a booking are deleted within a reasonable period. Reviews and photos stay until you ask us to remove them.

08

How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted staff access and secure password handling for our team. No online service is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information, such as card numbers, over chat or email. We will never ask you for your card PIN, OTP or password.

09

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your data, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.

Email Trippytrail.in@gmail.com from the address you used with us, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10

Children

Our trips are booked by adults. If a booking includes travellers under 18, it must be made by a parent or guardian, who consents to us handling the child’s details for the trip. We do not knowingly collect information from children for any other purpose.

11

Links to other sites

This website links to services like Instagram and WhatsApp. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website and our services grow. The “last updated” date at the top will always show the latest version. If we make an important change, we will say so on the website.

13
